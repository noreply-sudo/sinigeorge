About Mrs. Sini George

A pioneer reshaping nursing education in India

Mrs. Sini George is one of India's foremost nursing educators — a rare combination of clinical expertise, academic rigour, and entrepreneurial vision. With over 15 years in the field, she has trained thousands of nursing professionals and continues to push the boundaries of what healthcare education can look like in the digital age.

As the Chief Operating Officer of Ezi Nurse, she leads the development of India's most nurse-centric edtech platform, bringing evidence-based practice, interactive learning, and exam preparation tools to nurses across the country — many of whom are in underserved communities.

Career Journey

From bedside nurse
to edtech pioneer

Early Career
Clinical Nursing — Hospitals across Kerala & Karnataka
Began her journey as a staff nurse, developing deep clinical expertise in medical-surgical, critical care, and community health settings. This hands-on foundation became the bedrock of everything she would later teach.
Academic Transition
MSc Nursing & Faculty Role
Pursued postgraduate education in nursing, completing her MSc with distinction. Transitioned into nursing education as faculty, where she discovered her calling — teaching nurses to think, not just to follow protocols.
The Digital Turn
Co-founding & Leading Ezi Nurse
Recognised the massive gap in quality digital learning resources for Indian nurses. Co-founded Ezi Nurse and took on the role of COO — driving product vision, content creation, and national outreach to build India's leading nursing edtech platform.
Now
PhD Research, Policy Advocacy & National Impact
Currently pursuing her PhD while continuing to lead Ezi Nurse, engage with government stakeholders, speak at national conferences, and mentor thousands of nurses — committed to elevating the nursing profession at every level.
